REPRESENTATION BEYOND INTERFACE BOTTLENECKS

Beyond Token Time.

The model's representational space is much wider than the interfaces through which we make it communicate and advance. The bottleneck may come less from what the model can represent than from repeatedly forcing that space through tokens, one clock, one stream, and named modalities.

The central hypothesis is that internal representation and external interface should be separated. Internal state can remain rich, while discrete tokens, clocks, and modality-specific serialization are applied only when interaction requires them.

01 / THE THESIS

The interface is narrower than the representation.

A transformer computes high-dimensional continuous vectors at every layer. Many features and hypotheses can coexist, and there is no reason a vector must correspond to one word or one modality. Yet at the boundary between steps, this rich state is repeatedly pushed through a handful of strong constraints.

rich continuous state → interface bottleneck → next computation

This does not mean tokenization or autoregressive decoding was a mistake. On the contrary, they were extraordinarily effective scaffolds for scalable learning. But the scaffold that made intelligence trainable does not have to remain the native format in which intelligence ultimately computes.

What "Beyond Token Time" means"Token" stands for the broader act of collapsing internal state into a discrete interface symbol. "Time" stands for the assumption that all computation must advance on one fixed step counter. The argument is therefore broader than silent reasoning.
02 / ONE PATTERN, FOUR BOTTLENECKS

Token, time, modality, and seriality may be the same kind of constraint.

They look like different engineering choices, but each projects a wide internal representation into a coordinate system chosen for the interface.

BOTTLENECK 1

Discrete token

A continuous state is quantized into one vocabulary symbol, forcing semantic commitment.

BOTTLENECK 2

Fixed clock

Additional computation must follow a token position, fixed depth, or another single cadence.

BOTTLENECK 3

Named modality

Text, image, and audio appear as separate modes, inviting internal representation to inherit interface boundaries.

BOTTLENECK 4

Single serial stream

Entities, hypotheses, and plans that coexist must be serialized into one order.

CONSEQUENCE

Early commitment

Choices that could remain unresolved are fixed too early as token, order, modality, or one-stream state.

RELAXATION

Interface on demand

Keep internal state freer and project into an interface format only when interaction needs it.

representation ≠ serialization ≠ schedule ≠ modality
03 / TOKEN IS A USEFUL QUANTIZER

Chain-of-thought was a remarkably stable route to adaptive compute.

Explicit CoT can be viewed as a recurrent computation in which the reasoning content and the recurrent state carrier are the same discrete token stream.

h_t → reasoning token → E(token) → Transformer → h_{t+1}

This has enormous advantages. Expert traces provide supervised targets, while trace length naturally expresses variable reasoning depth. The model gains adaptive compute without first learning an invisible halting controller. In that sense, CoT is one of the most successful implementations of adaptive computation.

The cost is equally clear. State fed into the next step must cross a human-language semantic bottleneck. Even if multiple candidates, visual features, acoustic features, or plans coexist internally, the feedback path narrows to a token embedding.

The problem is exclusivity, not tokensTokens are excellent interfaces. The sharper question is whether they should be the only feedback channel for internal recurrence.
04 / TIME IS ALSO AN INTERFACE

Why should every process share one clock?

Current systems often conflate different notions of time: emitting another token, performing another model evaluation, and observing another event in the world.

CLOCK 1

Token time

Advances when another visible symbol is emitted.

CLOCK 2

Compute time

Advances when internal state receives another update.

CLOCK 3

World time

Advances when relevant external events occur.

token time ≠ compute time ≠ world time

An easy problem may need one pass; a difficult problem may deserve several passes at the same visible position. During conversation, speech generation can continue while audio arrives; vision may update at another cadence. Fixed timing is convenient for implementation, but there is little reason to treat it as the intrinsic time of intelligence.

So "Beyond Token Time" is not merely about thinking without tokens. It is about preventing the token sequence from monopolizing the clock of computation.

05 / MODALITY IS A LENS

Text, image, and audio are different observations of an underlying world.

A modality is closer to a sensor and sampling rule than to an ontological category inside the model.

observation in modality m = lens_m(world state)

An RGB camera samples selected wavelengths at a chosen spatial and temporal resolution. A microphone samples pressure changes. Korean and English encode the same events through different symbolic systems. Seen this way, text/image/audio are less like separate modes of intelligence and more like different lenses onto one underlying state.

Practical multimodal systems still need modality-specific encoders, tokenizers, and decoders. The danger is assuming those engineering boundaries are also the natural boundaries of internal representation. A single object's appearance, name, sound, motion, and affordances may be better represented as coupled properties of one state.

What modality-agnostic meansIt does not mean every sensor should share one tokenizer. Input and output adapters may remain specialized; the internal world representation need not inherit the adapter format.
06 / THE WORLD IS NOT A SINGLE STREAM

One output sequence does not imply one internal process.

A video frame contains many objects at once. A conversation scene preserves actor identity, voice, gaze, turn-taking, camera motion, and shared context simultaneously. The output has one timeline, but the represented world is not one chain.

Textual CoT has the same mismatch. To explain reasoning, we write "first consider hypothesis A, then B." Internally, evidence for both hypotheses may coexist. A serial trace is excellent for communication but need not be the required format of state.

one output stream ≠ one internal process

This motivates persistent multi-stream latent state. A stream here is not another model; it is a state component—entity, hypothesis, plan, dialogue state—that may update at its own cadence.

Let state be Z={z¹,z²,...,zᴷ} and update only components affected by an event. Modality adapters become observation and output lenses; tokens become one kind of readout; the compute scheduler can move independently according to task difficulty and world events.

Tokens are interfaces. Modalities are lenses. Time is a schedule. None of them has to be the native format of thought.
08 / CONCRETE RELAXATIONS

Null-token recurrence is one small mechanism for the larger thesis.

The null-token + gated-latent mechanism is not the architecture itself; it is a minimal way to relax both the token bottleneck and the coupling between token time and compute time.

Add an internal <NULL> action to the LM head. When null is selected, emit nothing to the user. Instead, mix the null embedding with a gated continuous vector from the previous state and run the transformer again.

No separate vertical recurrent stack is required. The ordinary autoregressive loop advances compute time while visible token time can remain still.

Combined with multi-stream state, the carry need not be one vector. A controller can mix several persistent components into the next computation.

Astra as a comparison

Public reporting describes Astra as using recurrent depth or a looped transformer, but the exact architecture is not public. The mechanism above is therefore not an attribution to Astra. It is an independent architectural hypothesis showing how functionally similar recurrence could be implemented inside an ordinary decode loop.

Monitorability

The trade-off follows naturally from the broader thesis. Text CoT happened to place internal compute and a human-readable trace on the same channel. Once representation is freed from the interface bottleneck, that alignment disappears. Monitorability must then become an explicit objective rather than an automatic property of the computation format.

09 / FALSIFIABLE TESTS

The larger representational freedom must earn its complexity.

"More continuous is better" is not a sufficient claim. Relaxing each bottleneck should produce measurable gains over compute-matched baselines.

Discrete bottleneck

Compare explicit CoT against null-token latent carry at matched FLOPs, then remove the carry to test whether gains come from representation or merely extra depth.

Time bottleneck

Compare fixed recurrent depth against learned variable recurrence and test whether compute allocation adapts to task difficulty.

Modality bottleneck

Observe the same event through text, image, and audio adapters; measure cross-modal consistency of shared latent state and direct action quality.

Seriality bottleneck

On tasks with independently evolving entities and hypotheses, compare a single-state baseline against persistent component state under matched compute.

Monitorability cost

As representational freedom increases, measure probe decodability, intervention predictability, and fidelity of sparse verbal checkpoints.

Killer testIf relaxing token, time, modality, or seriality bottlenecks produces no meaningful gain in accuracy, efficiency, robustness, or interruption handling under matched compute, the added latent architecture is not justified.
10 / PAPER

The position paper develops the architectural hypothesis and its concrete tests.

The paper centers the distinction between representation space and interface bottlenecks, then treats null-token recurrence and event-driven multi-stream state as concrete mechanisms for testing that hypothesis.
